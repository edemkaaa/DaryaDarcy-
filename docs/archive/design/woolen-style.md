Woolen typeface key characteristics observed from references:
- Hand-inked with visible brush/pen strokes, NOT thick/bold — actually quite light and elegant
- Letters have decorative swirling tails, especially on W, O, L, E, N
- The W has dramatic curling serifs at the top
- Letters are NOT uniform — each has unique hand-drawn character
- Thin to medium stroke weight (NOT bold/heavy)
- Organic, slightly wobbly baselines
- Inspired by Jean Jannon specimen from 1641
- Decorative botanical dividers with small leaves/diamonds
- Overall feel: hand-lettered calligraphy meets vintage printing press
- The D in Woolen style would have: decorative curling serif at top, thin elegant strokes, hand-inked texture
