# Font Reference Notes

## Woolen (by Magpie Paper Works)
- Hand-inked serif typeface
- Based on Jean Jannon specimen from 1641 (Imprimerie Royale)
- Inspired by freshly fallen snow and swags of mistletoe
- Characteristics: irregular hand-drawn serifs, organic ink texture, slightly wobbly baselines
- Warm, artisanal, vintage feel
- Capital letters are tall with decorative serifs that look hand-carved

## Sundried Linen (by The Little Typewriter)
- Elegant cursive/script companion font
- Hand-crafted calligraphic style
- Flowing, organic letterforms with natural variation
- Paired with a rustic serif (The Little Typewriter)
- Linen texture feel, warm and cottage-like

## Combined Heirloom Style
- Rustic, hand-drawn quality
- Organic imperfections that feel intentional
- Vintage/antique aesthetic
- Warm, cottage-core, farmhouse feel
- Serif letters with hand-inked texture
- Slightly irregular spacing and baselines
- Colors: typically dark ink on light/cream backgrounds, or cream/white on dark backgrounds

## For DARYADARCY Logo
- Should be in ALL CAPS serif style like Woolen
- Hand-inked, slightly irregular serifs
- Organic texture suggesting vintage letterpress
- Warm, rustic, heirloom quality
- White/cream color for overlay on dark images
- Transparent background PNG
